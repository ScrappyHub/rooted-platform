# ROOTED — Data Sovereignty & User Consent Law

Authority Level: Absolute Platform Law  
Enforcement: Database Policy + RLS + Admin Oversight  
Effective Date: First Public Launch

---

## 1. Data Ownership

All data belongs to the user or organization who created it.  
ROOTED is a steward — not an owner.

---

## 2. Explicit Consent Only

ROOTED may only collect:

- Data required to operate opted-in features
- Data required for platform security

ROOTED may NEVER:

- Sell user data
- Profile users for third parties
- Enrich shadow identity graphs
- Share data with brokers or political entities

---

## 3. User Rights

All users have the right to:

- View their data
- Correct their data
- Export their data
- Delete their data
- Revoke consent

Deletion requests must use:

`public.account_deletion_requests`

---

## 4. Zero Silent Tracking

ROOTED prohibits:

- Device fingerprinting
- Silent GPS harvesting
- Passive background scraping
- Identity-leaking analytics

Any tracking must be:
- Transparent
- Auditable
- Disablable

---

## 5. Life-First Technology Principle

ROOTED must never:
- Replace real human relationships
- Demand total attention
- Trap users in platform dependency

---

## 6. Violations

Violations trigger:

- Immediate system lockdown
- Forensic audit
- Permanent governance removal
- Legal escalation

Revenue does not override sovereignty.

---

## SENSITIVE ATTRIBUTES ARE NOT COLLECTED (2026-10-10)

ROOTED's database holds no religion, faith, age, birth date, ethnicity or similar personal attribute on any profile or account. Personal comfort settings that only change what a visitor sees, such as food filters (pork, alcohol, shellfish, dairy, nuts and so on) and holiday animations, live in the visitor's own browser, are never transmitted or stored by ROOTED, and are never visible to sellers or admins.

Religious content does not run through the agriculture vertical. User videos stay private to their owner. There is no public video publishing, and Kids Mode never shows user videos or user-made listings.
